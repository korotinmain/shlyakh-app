# The Path tab: constellation pages, route strip and the Milky Way map (constellation path, plan 5 of 7) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The Path tab replaces its placeholder.
- One page per constellation, swiped horizontally: the completed ones, the current one, and the next one (ahead). The rest stay in the fog.
- Each page has a header, the figure, an info card and a route strip of its neighbours:
  - completed pages are gold, with a seal "Складено <date>";
  - the current page shows XP to the next star, a bar and the ETA at your pace;
  - the page ahead is faint rings with an "opens later" line.
- A map button opens the Milky Way map: the galactic band on a real star chart, the visible constellations drawn at their real places and shapes, and fog beyond the next one.

**Architecture:**
- **Domain** (pure Dart):
  - `figureStatesFor`, the states of any constellation; `figureStates` becomes its current-constellation case;
  - `completionDates`, the day each constellation was completed, recomputed from daily steps;
  - `visibleConstellations`, the pages;
  - `galacticEquator` and `mapLayout`, the chart geometry.
- **Provider:** `pathProvider` combines the route and the days into a `PathView`.
- **Presentation:** `PathScreen` is a `PageView` of `ConstellationPage`s (reusing `ConstellationFigure` with a `done` style). `PathMapScreen` is a sub-route `/path/map`, drawn by a `CustomPainter` in a vertical scroll view.

**Tech Stack:** Flutter (`PageView`, `CustomPainter`, `SingleChildScrollView`), go_router 18 sub-route, Riverpod 3, intl, gen-l10n. No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-28-constellation-path-design.md`:
- Decisions: Path, Completed look (A + C), Current marker, Buttons, ETA;
- Components → Presentation → Path, and the Layout rule;
- Edge cases: main route finished, a new user with fewer than 7 days, very large text;
- Testing → Domain, Widgets.

Look: `full-concept-v4.html`, "«Шлях»" (current, completed and map phones), with the adopted reviewer changes: sentence case, no letter-spaced caps, no denominators.

## Global Constraints

- **Pages.** Constellations `0 … min(c + 1, last)` of the route, where `c` is the index of the next star's constellation (the last one when the whole route is lit).
  - The branch (Aql, Sct, Sgr) appears on its own once the main route is done, because it follows the main route.
  - The PageView opens on the current page.
  - There are no pages beyond the next one.
- **Page state.** `done` when the index is below `c` (or everything is lit), `current` when it equals `c`, `ahead` when it is above `c`.
- **Figure states for any constellation.**
  - A done constellation: every star lit.
  - An ahead one: every star ahead, except shared stars already lit by an earlier constellation.
  - The current one: as in plan 4.
- **Completion date.** The first local day on which the cumulative XP (the days sorted by date, each scored with `dailyXp`) reaches `xpToLight(k)`, where `k` is the number of route stars up to and including that constellation's last own star.
- **ETA.** `daysToNextStar(days: all days, today, xpLeft: xpForStar − xpIntoStar)`; the line is hidden when that is null.
- **Header.** Kicker, name (`title`) and subtitle, all in sentence case:

| State | Kicker | Subtitle |
|---|---|---|
| current | `pathNowHere` | `pathCompletedCount(n)` |
| done | `pathDone` (in the `done` colour) | `pathStarCount(own stars)` |
| ahead | `pathAhead` | `pathOpensLater` |

- **Info card** (matte glass, as the Today card):

| State | Content |
|---|---|
| current | `xpToNextStar`, a bar, `pathEta` |
| done | the seal (`pathSeal(date)` on the `seal` colour with `onSeal` text, a check icon), then `pathStarCount` |
| ahead | a lock icon and `pathOpensLater` |

- **Route strip.** The previous page's name, this page's name (bold, `onSky`) and the next page's name (`onSkyMuted`), over a dashed band line.
  - A slot with no page shows a faint dot: before the first page, or after the next page in the fog.
  - Tapping a neighbour's name animates the PageView to it.
- **Zones** (DESIGN "Layout"):
  - the header at the top (SafeArea);
  - the figure in the flexible zone;
  - the info card and the route strip at the bottom, ending above `FloatingTabBar.bottomClearance`.

  With very large text the page scrolls vertically inside itself; nothing is under the tab bar.
- **Tokens** (ARGB):

| Token | Dark | Light |
|---|---|---|
| `done` | `#F3D9A0` | `#8A5E0B` |
| `doneLine` | `#F7E2B4` at 90 % (`0xE6F7E2B4`) | `#8A5E0B` at 80 % (`0xCC8A5E0B`) |
| `doneGlow` | `#F3D9A0` at 30 % (`0x4DF3D9A0`) | transparent (`0x008A5E0B`) |
| `seal` | `#EBC57F` | `#8A5E0B` |
| `onSeal` | `#2A1D08` | `#FFFFFF` |

  Contrast: `done` against every `sky` colour ≥ 3; `onSeal` on `seal` ≥ 4.5.
- **Map geometry** (pure, in degrees):
  - `galacticEquator(l)`: the equatorial position of galactic longitude `l` on the galactic equator (J2000). North galactic pole α = 192.85948, δ = 27.12825; ℓ of the north celestial pole = 122.93192. The formulas:
    - δ = asin(cos δG · cos(ℓNCP − ℓ));
    - α = αG + atan2(sin(ℓNCP − ℓ), −sin δG · cos(ℓNCP − ℓ)), mod 360.
  - Chart: a plate carrée turned 90° clockwise, so north is to the right and increasing RA (unwrapped from an origin of RA 260°) goes up. This is a rotation, not a mirror, so shapes are not flipped.
  - Scale: `pxPerDeg = (width − 48) / (decMax − decMin)` over the declinations of every route constellation's centre, plus a margin of each one's `spanDeg / 2`.
  - Each constellation is drawn in its own true shape: its unit box (north up, east left) turned 90° clockwise, with a side of `spanDeg × pxPerDeg`, centred at its plate-carrée centre.
  - The band: the galactic equator sampled every 2° of ℓ, over the unwrapped RA range of the chart, stroked 20° wide in `onSkyMuted` at 12 % with a blur (`MaskFilter.blur`, sigma 12).
- **Map content:**
  - done constellations in gold, the current one with its states and marker, the next one with ahead rings; the others are not drawn.
  - Names sit under each visible figure (`footnote`, `onSkyMuted`; the current one in `onSky`, bold).
  - The band fades past the next constellation: its opacity falls to 0 over the next 20° of RA.
  - The scroll view opens with the current constellation centred.
- **Map header:** kicker `pathMapKicker`; title `pathCompletedCount(n)`; subtitle `pathLitStars(starsLit)` · `pathFog`.
- **Copy** (uk / en; plurals as ICU `{count, plural, …}`):

| Key | uk | en |
|---|---|---|
| `pathNowHere` | Зараз тут | You are here |
| `pathDone` | Складено | Complete |
